# Cyberpunk Burning Man

**Playable interactive 3D environment** of a cyberpunk Burning Man festival.

## Features
- Choose to play as **Dragon** or **Panda** (cyberpunk avatars)
- Free-roam first-person 3D world on the Playa
- The Man (cybernetic neon effigy) at center
- Floating holographic pyramids, fiber-optic forests, art cars
- Interactive NPCs with dialogue
- Dust particles, neon lights, stars
- Switch avatars with 1/2 keys
- E to interact

## How to Play
Open the live demo or open `index.html` locally (needs local server for modules).

WASD move, mouse look (click to lock), E talk, Space jump.

Built with Three.js by Dragon & Panda.
